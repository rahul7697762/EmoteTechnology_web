Client Identity

Your Business. Your WhatsApp Identity.

Emote Technology's service model is designed to respect and preserve each client's individual business identity and WhatsApp Business assets.

Client Business Identity

Each client business maintains its own business identity and WhatsApp Business assets. Emote Technology does not require all clients to use a single shared WhatsApp number.

Depending on the onboarding configuration, each client can connect its own eligible WhatsApp Business Account and phone number through the applicable Meta process.

The client's WhatsApp Business assets remain associated with the client's business environment. Emote Technology provides the technology and automation service required to operate the configured communication workflows.

What Emote Technology Provides

Emote Technology acts as a technology and automation provider. The platform and automation layer sit between the client's WhatsApp Business Account and the customer conversations — enabling efficient, structured communication.

Emote Technology does not represent itself as a business in customer conversations. The client's own business name, branding and contact information remain visible to customers.

Clients retain responsibility for their WhatsApp Business Account, their business identity and their compliance with applicable platform policies.

Technology Model

Built for Multiple Businesses

Emote Technology's technology-provider model is designed to support automation services for multiple independent businesses — without combining their identities or customer communication.

Each client business can have its own dedicated configuration, separate from other clients of Emote Technology. This allows businesses to use WhatsApp automation without combining the identities or customer communication of different businesses.

Privacy & Security

Data Handling & Security

Emote Technology takes the handling of business and customer information seriously. The following explains how information is used and protected.

How Emote Technology Handles Data

Emote Technology uses platform and customer information only as necessary to provide the requested technology and automation services.

Depending on the service configuration, information may include business account identifiers, WhatsApp-related account information, customer communication data and information required to operate automation workflows.

Access to information is limited to what is necessary for providing, maintaining and supporting the service.

Emote Technology does not use client information for unrelated purposes.

Customer Data & Client Responsibilities

Client businesses may process customer information through WhatsApp as part of their customer communication activities.

Client businesses remain responsible for ensuring that their customer communication and data processing practices comply with applicable laws, regulations, WhatsApp Business policies and their own privacy obligations.

Emote Technology provides technology services to support these workflows and processes information as necessary to provide the requested service.

Businesses using the service should ensure that their customers are appropriately informed about the use of WhatsApp for business communication, as required by applicable regulations and platform policies.

Security Measures

Emote Technology aims to protect business and customer information through appropriate technical and organisational measures.

These measures are intended to limit unauthorised access, maintain the integrity of configured services and support the secure operation of automation workflows.
